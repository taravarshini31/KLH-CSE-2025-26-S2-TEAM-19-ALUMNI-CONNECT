# 🎓 Alumni Connect

Alumni Connect is a web-based platform designed to connect students, alumni, and educational institutions on a single platform. It helps students interact with alumni based on their interests, skills, career goals, and organizations.

The platform supports networking, mentorship, career guidance, internships, job opportunities, and communication between students and alumni.

## 📌 Features

- 🔐 Student and Alumni Registration/Login
- 👤 Student and Alumni Profiles
- 🔎 Search Alumni by Skills, Interests, and Organization
- 🤝 Student-Alumni Networking
- 👨‍🏫 Mentorship and Career Guidance
- 💼 Job and Internship Opportunities
- 💬 Messaging between Students and Alumni
- 📅 Alumni Events and Workshops
- 🛠️ Admin Dashboard
- 📊 Manage Students, Alumni, Profiles, and Activities

## 🎯 Objectives

- To provide a common platform for students and alumni.
- To improve networking between students and alumni.
- To provide mentorship and career guidance to students.
- To share job and internship opportunities.
- To maintain organized alumni records.
- To support students in their placement and career development.

## 🧩 Modules

### 1. Authentication Module
Provides secure registration and login for students, alumni, and administrators.

### 2. Student Module
Students can create profiles, search for alumni, request mentorship, and view career opportunities.

### 3. Alumni Module
Alumni can create professional profiles, provide mentorship, share job opportunities, and interact with students.

### 4. Search and Matching Module
Students can find suitable alumni based on skills, interests, organizations, and career goals.

### 5. Mentorship Module
Students can send mentorship requests to alumni and receive career guidance.

### 6. Jobs and Internship Module
Alumni can post job and internship opportunities for students.

### 7. Messaging Module
Students and alumni can communicate through the platform.

### 8. Admin Module
Administrators can manage users, profiles, posts, events, and other system activities.
